Brand exploration · Direction A · Ink & Paper
AdaptFit
Write it down. Watch it add up.

AdaptFit is a training notebook that reads its own pages. It should feel like a good paper notebook in a quiet café: warm paper, black ink for the plan, blue ink for what you actually did, and a highlighter that only comes out when you've made progress.

The mark: ruled notebook lines, where the last line steps up, with a highlighter stroke behind the step.

01 · Principles

How it should feel

Four rules every screen is checked against.

01

Paper, not dashboard

Content sits on ruled lines and hairlines, not inside stacks of boxes. Cards only where something is genuinely a separate object.

02

Numbers are the hero

Loads and reps are set in a mono typeface, large and aligned. Everything else steps back.

03

Every colour has one job

Black ink = the plan and actions. Blue ink = what you did. Highlighter = progress. Pencil amber = check this. Red = errors.

04

Say less

Write like a margin note: short, plain, specific. Explanations live behind "Why?", never on the page by default.

03 · Typography

Three voices

A serif for titles (the notebook), the system font for reading (native and fast), and a mono for every number (the record). In the app, fonts are bundled so they work offline.

Titles · Fraunces
Upper Body

Screen titles, day headings (italic), the wordmark, and coach summaries. Soft, bookish, confident.

Reading · System (SF Pro / Roboto)
Lat pulldown

Exercise names, labels, buttons, body copy. Feels native on iPhone and Android, with no download.

Numbers · IBM Plex Mono
30 × 9,8,8

Every load, rep, date and stat. Digits line up in columns, and "the record" always looks like data.

Write like a margin note

Short, specific, warm. Numbers over adjectives. No exclamation marks and no hype.

07 · If approved

How it would roll out

1 · Foundations

New tokens (paper / ink / blue / highlighter / amber / red), bundled Fraunces and Plex Mono fonts (offline), light and night themes, new icon and splash screens.

2 · Components

Three button types, one selected state, ruled lists in place of card stacks, written-in statuses, a single date format.

3 · Screens

Plan and the workout note first, then the weekly update, Progress and Coach, then Import, Library and Settings. Copy is trimmed screen by screen.
